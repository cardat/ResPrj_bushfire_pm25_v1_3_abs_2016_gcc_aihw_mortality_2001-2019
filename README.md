# ResPrj_bushfire_pm25_abs_2016_sa1_aihw_mortality_2001-2019

This research project investigates the impact of bushfire-related fine particulate matter air pollution, with a diameter of 2.5 micrometres or less (PM<sub>2.5</sub>), on mortality risk in eight Australian GCCs from 2001 to 2019.

Data inputs:

- Greater Capital City Statistical Areas (GCCSAs) boundaries from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)
- Population counts at SA1-level from the 2016 Census (ABS)
- PM<sub>2.5</sub> exposure from Bushfire Smoke Exposures project (<https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/WQK4T>)
- Daily (non-accidental causes) mortality from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare

This repository is associated with the following journal article, in which details of methodology and results are presented:

> Hertzog, L.; Morgan, G. G.; Yuen, C.; Gopi, K.; Pereira, G. F.; Johnston, F. H.; Cope, M.; Chaston, T. B.; Vyas, A.; Vardoulakis, S.; Hanigan, I. C. Mortality Burden Attributable to Exceptional PMPM<sub>2.5</sub> Air Pollution Events in Australian Cities: A Health Impact Assessment. *Heliyon* **2024**, *10* (2), e24532. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e24532>.
